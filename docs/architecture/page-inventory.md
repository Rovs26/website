# WEB-2 Page Inventory and Page Contracts

Status: **Approved WEB-2 architecture register**

Reviewed: 2026-07-26

This is the canonical WEB-2 page-disposition register. It defines architecture
contracts, not final copy, route implementation, metadata, legal advice, or
publication approval. Route names are approved as architecture labels and
remain subject to the terminology gates in
`../governance/public-terminology.md`.

## Lifecycle vocabulary

| Lifecycle                           | Meaning                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Required for initial public release | The release is incomplete without this page, but publication still depends on its content and owner gates |
| Required before Play Store review   | Needed for store trust or policy review before that review begins                                         |
| Deferred                            | Intentionally postponed until a later need and owner exist                                                |
| Reserved                            | Namespace recorded, but no route, placeholder, link, sitemap entry, or indexable response exists          |
| Dormant                             | A formerly approved or published section withdrawn under an explicit archive or retirement decision       |
| Rejected                            | No current visitor need or operating model justifies the page; reconsider through a new decision          |
| Merged                              | The visitor need belongs in another page; no standalone route is created                                  |

## Status and publication baseline

- The dominant product status for the initial product pages is **Currently
  Testing**.
- No product capability is eligible for **Available Today** until a claim is
  explicitly approved in that category.
- **Planned** and **Future Vision** content is excluded from initial navigation
  and current-product sections.
- A reserved, dormant, deferred, merged, or rejected page does not return a
  public placeholder. It remains absent and unlinked.
- Every material statement must use an identifier from
  `../governance/public-claims-register.md` and an approved page occurrence in
  `../governance/page-claim-matrix.md`.

## Contract completeness gate

Before any planned page moves to implementation:

1. approve its disposition and proposed name;
2. assign the named owner roles;
3. approve every required claim occurrence and terminology choice;
4. resolve its blocking open decisions;
5. verify dominant and allowed product statuses;
6. approve its SEO/indexing scope without assuming a canonical host;
7. review accessibility, mobile, privacy, security, and staleness risks; and
8. record its later milestone acceptance.

WEB-2 creates none of these routes.
