How it works.

One day at the counter, from setting up your store to seeing the day's tubo.

  1. Step 1

    Create an account and set up your business.

    Add your store's name and choose English or Filipino. You can change the language at any time.

    {{ L.create }}
  2. Step 2

    Add your paninda.

    Enter each item with its price and its puhunan. KitaMo keeps the stock count and tags items that run low or run out.
  3. Step 3

    Sell at the counter.

    Tap items on the shelf, then take the payment. Sakto and the ₱200, ₱500 and ₱1,000 keys work out the sukli. Tag GCash, Maya or bank payments with their reference number.

  4. Step 4

    Record gastos and utang.

    Set upa, kuryente, tubig, wifi and sahod as repeating bills. Under Utang, Sa iyo lists customers who owe you and Utang mo lists suppliers you owe.

  5. Step 5

    Check Kita at the end of the day.

    Kita shows your tubo for today, this week and this month, a 4-week trend, and the items that earned the most.

    Screens show the KitaMo web app with sample data.

What KitaMo doesn't do.

  • It needs internet.
  • It records payments but doesn't move money.
  • The receipt is not an official BIR receipt.
  • There are no staff logins or multiple stalls yet.
