About KitaMo.

KitaMo is a web app for Filipino micro-sellers: sari-sari stores, karinderias, food stalls, market stalls and home businesses. You sell at the counter, track stock, log bills and spoilage, keep utang both ways, and see what you really earned each day.

Why

Your lista, with the math done.

Most owners keep a paper lista and have never used accounting software. KitaMo keeps the same list and does the adding and subtracting, so the day's tubo is ready when you close.

Tubo = Benta − Puhunan − Bayarin − Sira
