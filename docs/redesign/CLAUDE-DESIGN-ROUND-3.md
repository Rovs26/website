Round 2 landed every fix. The homepage is close to final. This round has three
small layout fixes and the three parts of round 2 that did not arrive.

## Fixes to the homepage

1. **The header button wraps in Filipino at 360 px.** "Gumawa ng libreng
   account" breaks onto two lines in the header next to Menu.
   - Below 400 px, show only the mark and Menu in the header. The hero button
     sits right below it.
   - Once the visitor scrolls past the hero, a compact "Create free account"
     may appear in the header.
   - Show 360 px in Filipino.
2. **"Made for the counter": the phone covers the tablet's cart.** It hides the
   Total (₱183.00) and the Checkout button of T2. Move the phone so the whole
   cart pane stays visible, as you did for the hero.
   - Design this section a second way too: a single wide photo slot (16:9
     desktop, 4:5 phone) with the four lines beside or below it. A photo of the
     tablet on a real counter is being made for it.
3. **The daily-routine phones are too small at 390 px.** At about 118 px wide,
   nothing on the screen can be read. Make them at least 160 px wide, or show a
   larger crop of the top half of each screen.

## Top earners

The T1 plate's top-earner profits disagree with the costs in P3. At ₱75 price
and ₱62 cost, Softdrinks 1.5L × 14 is **₱182.00**, not ₱266.00. At ₱18 and ₱14,
Pancit canton × 37 is **₱148.00**, not ₱185.00. Bigas 1 kg × 12 at ₱96.00 is
right. Use these figures wherever T1 appears.

## Missing from round 2

### 1. The three subpages

Design each at 390 × 844 and 1440 × 900, in 1b. Reuse the plates. The copy is a
draft for the owner.

**How it works.** The daily loop, one step per block with its plate:

| Step | What the seller does                        | Plate  |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| 1    | Create an account and set up your business. | —      |
| 2    | Add your paninda.                           | P3     |
| 3    | Sell at the counter.                        | P1, P2 |
| 4    | Record gastos and utang.                    | P5, P4 |
| 5    | Check Kita at the end of the day.           | T1, P6 |

Close with a short, plain "What KitaMo doesn't do" list:

- it needs internet;
- it records payments but doesn't move money;
- the receipt is not an official BIR receipt;
- there are no staff logins or multiple stalls yet.

**For sellers.** One short block each for:

- sari-sari store;
- karinderia;
- food stall;
- market stall;
- home business.

Each block says which parts of KitaMo that seller leans on. For example, a
karinderia logs leftovers as sira and tracks daily bills. Don't mention recipe
costing; it is not in the web app. Leave a 4:5 photo slot on the karinderia
block.

**About.**

- What KitaMo is.
- Why: "your lista, with the math done".
- Where it stands: the web app is free while in testing, and an offline
  Android version is Currently Testing.
- A link to Support at app.kitamo.online/support.
